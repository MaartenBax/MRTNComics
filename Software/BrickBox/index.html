BrickBox

Build. Light. Render.

A LEGO-inspired 3D building sandbox focused on unrestricted creation, lighting and rendering.

Explore BrickBoxDevelopment

Concept

Build without boundaries.

BrickBox is a creative sandbox for digital building: place parts, shape scenes, tune materials, light the result and render it without a prescribed way to build.

Key features

01

Unrestricted building

Build freely with a large part library, snapping and transform tools.

02

Materials & lighting

Control color, roughness, metallic, emissive materials, HDRI and scene lights.

03

Realtime rendering

Work directly in a responsive viewport while shaping the scene.

04

Path tracing

Switch to physically based rendering when the final image matters.

Build & render

From idea to image.

The same project moves from rough construction to lighting, composition and final presentation.

Development

Still being built.

BrickBox is an active personal project. New building, library, rendering and workflow systems continue to evolve.

One personal creative space for games, comics, design, motion and visual experiments.
