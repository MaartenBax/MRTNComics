About

Echoing Horizon is a handcrafted experiential puzzle game focused on atmosphere, observation and discovery. Every puzzle is deliberately designed by hand, encouraging players to slow down, experiment and understand the world around them.

Philosophy

Built around curiosity.

01

Handcrafted

Every space and puzzle is created by hand with purpose and intention.

02

Observation

Progress comes from looking carefully and understanding the environment.

03

Atmosphere

The world is designed to communicate through light, scale and silence.

04

Discovery

No waypoints. No noise. Just the player, the puzzle and the world.
